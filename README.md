## 3. Post-Care Regimen (Microblading)

Proper aftercare is essential to support healing and achieve the best possible results.

### What to Expect

- Pigment oxidizes immediately after the procedure.
- Brows may initially appear darker before peeling and fading.
- This is a normal part of the healing process.

### Aftercare Instructions

1. Apply thin coats of **Aquaphor twice daily**. If you have oily skin, apply once daily.
2. **DO NOT** pick, rub, or scratch dry or flaking areas.
3. Keep brows dry until flaking is complete.
4. Avoid sweating, heavy exercise, hot tubs, and swimming until flaking is complete.
5. Avoid direct sun exposure and tanning for **3 weeks**.

Following these instructions helps support the healing process and maintain pigment retention.
